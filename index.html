How it works

You ask one question and get back a shortlist. Here's what happens with a real one.

1

You say what you want

Out loud, in your own words. Mention your budget, a price range or a type of product if you have one in mind. If you don't, that's fine too.

“I've got about £1,500 and I'd like to sell something for pets. Nothing heavy.”
2

Veyra does the digging

It looks at what people are buying on Amazon, how crowded each market is, what Amazon takes in fees and what factories charge to make it.

3

You get a short list, explained

Each product comes with what you'd keep from a sale and the reason it made the cut. Any number you don't recognise has a plain-English explanation next to it.
